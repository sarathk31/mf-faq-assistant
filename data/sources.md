## Conflicts found and how they were resolved

1. **Liquid fund exit load.** HDFC's general blog (S21) says liquid funds have no exit load. HDFC Liquid Fund's own KIM (S08) and scheme page (S04) show a day-wise exit load up to Day 6. The scheme-specific document wins, and the blog is used only for definitions.
2. **Liquid fund exit load completeness.** The scheme page (S04) lists Day 1 to Day 6 but omits "Day 7 onwards Nil". The KIM (S08) states the full schedule, so it is cited for this fact.
3. **ELSS scheme name.** The KIM and SID (dated 21 Nov 2025) use "HDFC ELSS Tax Saver". The Fund Facts sheet (S13) says the name changed to "HDFC ELSS - Tax Saver Fund" from 26 Aug 2026. The current name is used, with the old name kept as an alias.
4. **TER date.** Scheme pages show TER without a date. The values were cross-checked against HDFC's TER report (S18): all eight Direct/Regular values match the 31 Oct 2026 entries.
5. **Outdated tax figure.** The capital-gains guide (S20, updated 3 Jul 2026) mentions an LTCG exemption "up to Rs 1 lakh". Tax rates are out of scope and that sentence is excluded.
