# Product selection (Checkpoint 1)

Scores are judgments from each product's public pages (1 = weak, 5 = strong), not measurements.
Weights: retail MF journey 25%, official help docs 20%, public product info 15%, focused FAQ use case 15%,
buildable without private APIs 15%, fits the deadline 10%.

| Product | What it is | Info | Journey | Help docs | Focus | No API | Scope | Weighted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Groww** | Large retail direct-plan MF platform with a public MF help centre | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **4.80** |
| Kuvera | Direct-plan MF platform, Freshdesk help centre | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4.15 |
| INDmoney | Super-app; MF is one of many journeys | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3.80 |
| PowerUp Money | Paid MF advisory plus tracking; advice is the product | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3.20 |
| Wealth Monitor (Dezerv) | Portfolio-review tool that flags funds and recommends changes | 2 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 2.60 |

**Choice: Groww.** Its users match the brief (retail, direct plans), and its help centre shows which factual questions
recur. Advice-led products (PowerUp, Wealth Monitor) conflict with a no-advice assistant.

**Assumptions and limits:** the assistant is not integrated with Groww. Groww's scheme pages are distributor content and are
never cited; all facts come from the AMC or SEBI. "Wealth Monitor" was taken to mean Dezerv's tool.
