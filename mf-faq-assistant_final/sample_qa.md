## 1. What is the exit load for HDFC Liquid Fund?

- **Category:** Direct factual (classified as `FACTUAL_SCHEME_QUERY`)
- **Response type:** Factual
- **Assistant:** For HDFC Liquid Fund, the exit load on units redeemed or switched out is 0.0070% on Day 1, 0.0065% on Day 2, 0.0060% on Day 3, 0.0055% on Day 4, 0.0050% on Day 5 and 0.0045% on Day 6, and Nil from Day 7 onwards, as a % of redemption proceeds (KIM dated 21 Nov 2025). Day 1 is the allotment day if the application and funds are received within the cut-off time, otherwise the day after allotment.
- **Source:** [KIM - HDFC Liquid Fund dated November 21, 2025](https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/KIM/2026-06/KIM%20-%20HDFC%20Liquid%20Fund%20dated%20November%2021%2C%202025.pdf)

## 4. How can I get a capital gains statement?

- **Category:** Investor service (classified as `GENERAL_INVESTOR_SERVICE_QUERY`)
- **Response type:** Factual
- **Assistant:** You can get a capital gains statement from the registrars CAMS (camsonline.com) or KFintech (kfintech.com) using the PAN and email ID registered with your investments, or from each fund house's website. - **Source:** [How to Get a Capital Gain Statement for Mutual Fund schemes in India](https://www.hdfcfund.com/learn/blog/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-fund-schemes-india)

## 6. What is a riskometer?

- **Category:** Educational (classified as `EDUCATIONAL_QUERY`)
- **Response type:** Factual
- **Assistant:** A riskometer (Risk-o-meter) shows a mutual fund scheme's risk level. SEBI requires six levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High and Very High.
- **Source:** [SEBI Circular - Product Labeling in Mutual Fund schemes: Risk-o-meter (SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2020/197)](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2020/circular-on-product-labeling-in-mutual-fund-schemes-risk-o-meter_47796.html)
- **Last updated from sources: 4 Nov 2026**
